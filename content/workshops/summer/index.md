Summer is the season of light, movement and aliveness. The Summer workshops are for adults going through change, whether an ending or a new chapter, and for anyone ready to rediscover what truly lights them up.
{{< /lead >}}

## 1. Separation
### **The Art Of Navigating It**

{{< alert icon=" " cardColor="#f4a261" textColor="#1f2937" >}}

Whether it's a relationship, a friendship, a career, or a version of yourself - endings are beginnings in disguise. 

Learn to move through separation with grace, emotional intelligence, and a perspective that opens rather than closes doors.

{{< /alert >}}

## 2. Finding Your Passion
### **Discovering Who You Are**

{{< alert icon=" " cardColor="#2a9d8f" textColor="#f8fbff" >}}

Summer is the season of aliveness - and this workshop dares you to ask: what truly lights me up? 

Through guided exploration, mindset work, and discovery exercises, you'll uncover your authentic passions and learn to pursue them without apology.
