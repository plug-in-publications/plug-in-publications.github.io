## Intention Setting

### Beginning the year from the inside out

{{< alert icon=" " cardColor="#1f3b5b" textColor="#f8fbff" >}}

Before the new year begins, we get quiet and we get honest.

This workshop is not about goals or resolutions - it is about setting intentions that are truly yours. Rooted in clarity, aligned with your values, and offered with trust to the season ahead. You will leave knowing not just what you want - but why, and who you are becoming in the pursuit of it.

## Self Love & Self Worth

### Checking in with the one who matters most

{{< alert icon=" " cardColor="#4f6d8a" textColor="#f8fbff" >}}

How long has it been since you truly checked in with yourself - not your productivity, not your roles, not what others need from you - but you? 

This workshop is a gentle and honest return to yourself. We explore where your sense of worth has been living, rebuild it from the inside, and practice the daily act of choosing yourself not out of selfishness but out of wholeness.
