Our children's books help young readers care for their inner world as much as their outer one.
Each story gives kids simple, practical tools they can use every day, and gives parents and
teachers a gentle way to start meaningful conversations.

- **Mindfulness and calm**: [Layla Listens Inside](/books/layla-listens-inside/) shows children how to slow a busy mind and find peace within.
- **Positive thinking and self-talk**: [Zahra and the Garden of Thoughts](/books/zahra-and-the-garden-of-thoughts/) and [Mara Talks Kindly to Herself](/books/mara-talks-kindly-to-herself/) build confidence and self-worth.
- **Gratitude**: [Nora and the Thankful Star](/books/nora-and-the-thankful-star/) is a bedtime story about the magic of being thankful.
- **Cultural diversity and kindness**: [The Story Inside My Lunchbox](/books/the-story-inside-my-lunchbox/) and [Frybread and Date](/books/frybread-and-date/) celebrate the traditions every family brings to the table.
- **Money and business skills**: [Our Family Means Business!](/books/our-family-means-business/) is a hands-on workbook that teaches kids to earn, save, price and keep a ledger.
