Amani Aseel began writing the stories she wished she could read to her own children: stories that
nurture emotional strength, inner peace, and appreciation for cultural diversity. When she couldn't find
enough books that spoke to the heart and mind together, she decided to write them herself.

Her stories inspire children to care for their inner world just as they care for their outer one,
planting seeds of mental health, courage, and kindness that continue to grow throughout their lives.

When she's not writing, Amani can be found baking warm focaccia bread with her daughters, with a little
help from her loving husband, who proudly handles the taste-testing. Together, they fill their home with
laughter, love, and the comforting scent of olive oil and rosemary.

Amani's mission is simple: to help every child feel seen, supported, and strong from the inside out.
