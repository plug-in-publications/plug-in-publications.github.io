Maurizio Dusi is an IT Director with decades of experience in the field. He has worked in Italy, Germany,
and the US as a researcher in company labs, a software developer, and a leader of IT teams. He is also an
unapologetic tax and finance nerd, a father of two, and, as his wife says, a loving husband.

He has a habit of explaining hard ideas with things from everyday life. It produced
*Haskell for the Gloriously Lazy*, a humorous guide to functional programming, and
*Our Family Means Business!*, a kid's guide to earning, owning, and growing money. He lives in Southern
California with his family, where he learns something new every day from his curious kids.
